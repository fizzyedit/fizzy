# Licensing

## Fizzy is free, forever

Fizzy is MIT-licensed (`LICENSE`): the application, the framework in `app/`, the plugin SDK in
`sdk/`, `core/`, and the bundled plugins in `plugins/`. Every version ever released stays MIT.
That grant cannot be withdrawn, by the maintainers or by anyone who comes after them.

## Build what you want on it, commercial work included

- **Plugins** may carry any license, closed-source and paid included. A plugin meets Fizzy only
  through the SDK, and the SDK asks nothing of it beyond keeping the MIT notice with any copy of
  the SDK's own source it ships.
- **Applications** built on Fizzy as a library (`fizzy.buildApp`) may be commercial and
  closed-source, under the same condition.
- **The plugin store** may list free and paid plugins alike; each listing states its license.

## What the project commits to

- Fizzy itself, the editor and the framework, will never become paid, open-core, or split into
  a "community edition". No feature of Fizzy is held back for a paid tier; paid work belongs in
  plugins, made by whoever wants to make them, the maintainers included.
- The SDK will never require a license key, an account, or a payment to load a plugin.
- Contributions come in under the license they go out under: MIT, with no contributor license
  agreement. No single party collects the right to relicense other people's work.

## The name

"Fizzy" and the Fizzy logo are trademarks of Colton Franklin. Forks are welcome under another
name; what you may call a build of Fizzy, and how, is in `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## History

Fizzy was MIT from 2022 until May 2026, GPL-3.0 from then until this file was added, and MIT
again since. Code in the GPL-3.0 releases was written by Colton Franklin, apart from one
contribution whose author agreed to its relicensing, so the same code is offered under MIT too.
