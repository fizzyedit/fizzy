app: chrome corner_buttons option

Chrome gains corner_buttons (default true; -Dchrome-corner-buttons=false). It sets
Layout.corner_buttons, and Region.cornerButton draws no orb, glyph, ring or handle when
it is false. A drag begun elsewhere still ticks and shows its drop zones.
